PROCESS / 01—06

CLARITY IS
PART OF THE BUILD.

The finished result matters, but so do the months of decisions, communication and coordination required to get there.

Northline uses a structured sequence so scope, responsibilities, milestones and handover remain understandable throughout the build.

01 / 06

Site consultation

Understand the property, the desired outcome, the current information available and whether plans already exist.

02 / 06

Scope & quotation

Define included work, key exclusions, assumptions and commercial terms clearly enough for the client to compare the right things.

03 / 06

Planning

Build the programme, coordinate procurement and identify decisions that could affect timing or cost.

04 / 06

Construction

Run the site under a named project lead and communicate progress through agreed milestones.

05 / 06

Quality review

Inspect completed work, identify outstanding items and resolve them before final handover.

06 / 06

Handover

Review the completed project with the client and close out the agreed scope and documentation.

COMMUNICATION

Know who to ask.

A named project lead makes responsibility visible.

VARIATIONS

Changes are discussed.

Additional work should not arrive as an unexplained surprise.

HANDOVER

Finish deliberately.

A snag review gives the end of the project a defined process.
