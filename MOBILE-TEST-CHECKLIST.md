# Northline phone test

Test the live site on the Samsung A13 after deployment.

1. Open the homepage on mobile data and Wi-Fi.
2. Confirm the hero appears quickly and the Start Your Project button is usable immediately.
3. Scroll through the full Blueprint-to-Built sequence slowly, then quickly. Check for jumps, blank frames or freezing.
4. Confirm the portrait construction images fill the phone naturally and the text remains in front.
5. Open and close the mobile menu.
6. Drag the before/after comparison left and right.
7. Swipe through Featured Projects.
8. Complete all five quote steps without submitting personal information.
9. Check Services, Projects, Process, About and Contact pages for horizontal scrolling or clipped text.
10. Rotate the phone once and confirm the layout recovers correctly.

Send a screenshot of anything that looks wrong and note whether scrolling felt smooth, slightly heavy, or laggy.
