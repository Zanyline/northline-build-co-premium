WHAT AN EXTENSION
NEEDS TO GET RIGHT.

A good extension should feel considered as part of the original home — structurally, visually and practically.

Northline coordinates the old and new portions of the home as one project, with the interface between them treated as a key part of the build.

01

Start with the existing home

The project begins by understanding what is already built and what the new space must solve.

02

Plans matter

Whether drawings are approved or still being developed, the next steps depend on understanding the current design information early.

03

Match old and new

Structure, rooflines, levels and finishes need to feel considered rather than attached as an afterthought.
